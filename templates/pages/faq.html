Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Click "Create account" in the sidebar and fill in your details to get started.

How do I add a prescription?

Go to Prescriptions → Add Prescription and fill in the medicine details including dosage and schedule.

Reminders

How do medicine reminders work?

Enable medicine alerts from the sidebar. You'll receive reminders based on your prescription schedules.

Yes, we take data security seriously. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
